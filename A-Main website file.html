Top Attractions

Edinburgh Castle

Edinburgh Castle (Scottish Gaelic: Caisteal Dhùn Èideann) is a historic castle in Edinburgh, Scotland. It stands on Castle Rock, which has been occupied by humans since at least the Iron Age.The castle, in the care of Historic Environment Scotland, is Scotland's most (and the United Kingdom's second most) visited paid tourist attraction.

Isle of Skye

The Isle of Skyeis the largest and northernmost of the major islands in the Inner Hebrides of Scotland.The island's peninsulas radiate from a mountainous hub dominated by the Cuillin, the rocky slopes of which provide some of the most dramatic mountain scenery in the country.

Scottish Culture

Music & Traditions

Scotland is internationally known for its traditional music, often known as Scottish folk music.Traditional Scottish music is closely associated with the bagpipes which is credited as having a prominent role in traditional music originating from the country.

Festivals

Scotland hosts on average 200 festivals per year, ranging from cultural, musical and arts. The Edinburgh Fringe Festival is the world's largest arts festival, and the country is also renowned for its hosting and organisation of sporting festivals and cultural events.

Travel Information

When to Visit

The best times to visit Scotland are spring (late March to May) and fall (September to November) for a balance of milder weather, fewer crowds, and beautiful scenery.

Transport

The two main ways to get around Scotland is either by train or by car. However, if you want to travel extensively around the country, a rental car is the way to go.

Weather

The weather in Scotland tends to be quite moderate and changeable but is rarely extreme.There’s no bad time of year to visit Scotland, with plenty to see and do regardless of the elements.
